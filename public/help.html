Receipt Confirmation — User Guide

CCH · Powered by Inhance & Flowgear

Pricing & Warehouse Staff

This app receives inbound receipt confirmations from the Flowgear workflow, lets you enter the price per unit (Rate) for each stock line, and then posts the completed receipt to the ERP. It must be opened from inside the Flowgear Console — if you see an orange warning banner, contact your administrator.

Step-by-step Workflow

1

Refresh List

Click Refresh list to pull the latest pending receipts from Flowgear. Amber rows have been edited (rates saved, not yet posted); green rows are fully priced and ready to post.

›
2

Select a Receipt

Click a row to open it. If it was previously partially priced, your saved rates will reload automatically.

›
3

Enter Rates

Type the Rate (R/UOM) for every line. The Order Price calculates automatically. You can navigate away at any time — your progress is saved.

›
4

Post to ERP

Click Post to ERP, type Y to confirm. The receipt is sent to the ERP and removed from the list.

›
5

Repeat

Click OK — load next receipt or Refresh to move on to the next order.

Field Reference

HReceipt Header Fields

  • Company — The CCH legal entity for this receipt.
  • Receipt No. — Unique inbound receipt identifier.
  • Supplier — The vendor delivering the goods.
  • Order type — e.g. Purchase Order, Return.
  • Probill — The carrier's freight bill number.
  • Reference — Additional cross-reference number.

LLine Item Fields

  • Line — ERP line number (read-only).
  • Item code — Stock-keeping unit identifier.
  • Description — Item name from the ERP.
  • Pack Size / Lot — Pack configuration and lot / batch number.
  • Qty — Quantity received (units).
  • Net weight (kg) — Total net weight received.
  • UOM — Unit of measure: KG or other (e.g. EA, CTN).
  • Rate (R/UOM) — ★ Price per unit. You enter this.
  • Order price — Calculated automatically (see formula below).

FOrder Price Formula

When UOM = KG
Order Price = Net weight × Rate
All other UOMs (EA, CTN, etc.)
Order Price = Quantity × Rate

Lines with UOM = KG are priced by weight. Everything else is priced by quantity.

SFilter & Search

  • Current user — Multi-select to show only orders locked to specific users.
  • Company — Partial text match on company name.
  • Probill — Partial match on probill number.
  • Receipt no. — Partial match on receipt number.
  • From / To date — Shorten the list to a date range. All records are always loaded from Flowgear on Refresh — the date filter only hides rows outside the range without reloading data.
  • Refresh list — Reload all records from Flowgear (filters do not affect what is fetched).
  • Clear filters — Reset all filters (appears when filters are active).

Order Capture State

Each order row is colour-coded to show its pricing progress. Your rates are automatically saved when you navigate away, so you can work across multiple receipts without losing data.

Colour State Meaning
White / grey Unpriced No rates have been entered yet.
Amber Edited Rates have been saved but the receipt has not yet been posted. The name of the last person to edit it appears directly below the badge. Previously entered rates reload automatically when you reopen the order.
Green Ready to Post All lines have rates. The receipt can be posted to the ERP immediately.

The lock is released automatically when you navigate to a different order — you never need to manually unlock a record.

Receipt Locking

To prevent two users editing the same receipt at the same time, the app uses an automatic locking system. You do not need to manually lock anything — it happens when you start editing a rate. The lock is released as soon as you click a different order in the list.

Situation What you see What to do
No lock holder — (dash) in Current user column Receipt is free — open and edit normally.
You hold the lock Your username shown in Current user column Edit rates and post as normal.
Another user holds the lock Yellow banner + their name in the column Wait for them to finish and release the lock, then click Refresh.
Another browser tab has this receipt open Yellow banner "Another browser tab…" Close the other tab. The lock releases automatically within ~2 minutes.
Your session expired Red banner "Not signed in" Sign back in to the Flowgear Console and click Refresh.

Tips & Common Questions

💡

Post button is greyed out

Every line must have a Rate greater than 0 before posting is enabled. Check for any blank Rate fields.

⚖️

Hold Code details toggle

The "Show Hold Code details" checkbox splits lines by hold code for review only. Turn it off to enter rates — you cannot edit rates in hold code view.

📋

Averaged rates (★ asterisk)

When grouped lines have different rates, the displayed rate is an average (marked ★). Click into the field to set a single rate across the group.

🔄

Receipt disappears after posting

That's expected — a successfully posted receipt is removed from the pending list. Click "OK — load next receipt" or Refresh to continue.

📅

Date sort

Click the Date column header in the Orders list to toggle between oldest-first (▲) and newest-first (▼). Your preference is remembered between sessions.

◀

Collapsing the Orders list

Click the ‹ / › toggle between the Orders list and the editor to hide the list and give the receipt editor more space.

🔎

Target & Source payload buttons

At the bottom of the editor, "View target payload" and "View source payload" show the raw JSON sent to and received from Flowgear. Useful for troubleshooting with your administrator.

❓

Reopening this tour in the app

Click the ? button in the top navigation bar of the app at any time to restart the interactive tour.
